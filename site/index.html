        Data and methods

        • Expression data: , analysed with DESeq2 (Love, Huber & Anders, Genome Biology 2014) via pydeseq2.
        • Immune processes: ImmPort gene lists, curated from GO "immune system process" and Reactome "Immune System".
        • Immune cell markers: Human Immune Cell Atlas (resources/human_immune_cell_atlas.docx in the repository). Its panels are candidate markers, not validated signatures.
        • Current gene symbols: HGNC.
